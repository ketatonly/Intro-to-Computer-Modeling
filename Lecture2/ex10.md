### Introduce subnormal numbers within our toy floating point system. List all of them. 

### Answer:

The toy number system:

$\mathbb{F}_{2,3}^{-1,2}$

So, all the numbers are: $\quad x = \pm (b_0.b_1b_2)_2 \times 2^e \quad$ where e is between -1 and 2.

We need to find all the subnormal numbers, that means b_0 must be zero while at least one of b_1 or b_2 is nonzero, also, e must be the minimum, which is -1.

$\quad x = \pm (0.b_1b_2)_2 \times 2^{-1}$

Numbers are:

$ x = \pm (0.01)_2 \times 2^{-1} = \frac{1}{4} * \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{8} = \pm 0.125$

$ x = \pm (0.10)_2 \times 2^{-1} = \frac{1}{2} * \frac{1}{2} = \frac{1}{4} = \pm 0.25$

$ x = \pm (0.11)_2 \times 2^{-1} = (\frac{1}{2} + \frac{1}{4}) * \frac{1}{2} = \frac{3}{8} = \pm 0.375$
