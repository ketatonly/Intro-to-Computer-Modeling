### Find the exact number of normal real numbers represented by the general set of floating point numbers $\mathbb{F}_{\beta, p}^{\check{e}, \hat{e}} $ in terms of its parameters. 

### Answer:

Floating numbers in these set are:

$x = \pm (b_0.b_1b_2...b_{p-1})_\beta \times {\beta}^e$

Since we are counting normal numbers, b_0 must be nonzero, so $1\leq b_0 \lt \beta \quad$ which gives us $\beta -1$ possibilities, and for the rest of the bs, we have $\beta^{p-1}$ possibilies, multiplied by $|\check{e}-\hat{e}|+1$ number or combinations.

We need to multiply all this possibilies and multiply them by 2 for plus minus numbers:

$ (\beta-1) \cdot (\beta^{p-1}) \cdot (|\check{e}-\hat{e}|+1) \cdot 2 $
