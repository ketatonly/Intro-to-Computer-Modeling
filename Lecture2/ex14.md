### Show that if x is a real number within the range of normalized floating point numbers representable in the computer the relative error caused by rounding, |x − O(x)|/|x|, is bounded by $β^{-(p – 1)}$ in the case of rounding by truncation, and by $\frac{1}{2}β^{-(p – 1)}$ when rounding to nearest. 

### Answer:
We merely have to rewrite the errors given in the reader from base 10 to base $\beta$:

The reader gives:

## $|relerr(x)| = \frac{|abserr(x)|}{|x|} \leq \frac{5\cdot 10^{e-p}}{10^e}$ 

This one is for base 10, but for base $\beta$ it will be:

## $|relerr(x)| = \frac{|abserr(x)|}{|x|} \leq \frac{\frac{\beta}{2} \cdot \beta^{e-p}}{\beta^e} = \frac{\beta^{e-p+1}}{2\cdot \beta^e} = \frac{\beta^{-p+1}}{2}$  

Which is for rounding to nearest. And now for the rounding by truncation, the reader gives:

## $|\frac{x - O(x)}{x}| \leq |\frac{g\cdot 10^{e-p}}{1\cdot10^e}| \lt \frac{10\cdot 10^{e-p}}{1\cdot10^e} = 10^{-(p-1)}$ 

For base $\beta$ this will be:

## $|\frac{x - O(x)}{x}| \leq |\frac{g\cdot \beta^{e-p}}{1\cdot\beta^e}| \lt \frac{\beta\cdot \beta^{e-p}}{1\cdot\beta^e} = \beta^{-p+1}$
