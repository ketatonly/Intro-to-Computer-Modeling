### How many denormalized real numbers are represented by the set of floating point numbers $\mathbb{F}_{\beta, p}^{\check{e}, \hat{e}} $? Express this number in terms of β, p, ě and ê

### Answer:

Floating numbers in these set are:

$x = \pm (b_0.b_1b_2...b_{p-1})_\beta \times {\beta}^e$

Since we are counting subnormal numbers, b_0 must be zero and at least one of the bs must be nonzero, so $b_0 = 0 \quad$. For the rest of the bs, they can be any $\beta$ giving us $\beta^{p-1}$ possibilities, but we need to subtract one case where every b is zero so we need -1. For the subnormal numbers, the exponent is forced to be the minumum, which gives us only one possiblity: $\quad e = \check{e}$.

We need to multiply all this possibilies and multiply them by 2 for plus minus numbers:

$(\beta^{p-1} - 1) \cdot 2 $
