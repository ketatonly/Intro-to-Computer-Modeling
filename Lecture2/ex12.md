### Find largest and smallest positive subnormal numbers, $N_{max}^s$ and $N_{min}^s$.

### Answer:

$\mathbb{F}_{\beta, p}^{\check{e}}$

So, all the numbers are: $\quad x = \pm (b_0.b_1b_2...b_{p-1})_\beta \times {\beta}^{\check{e}} \quad$.

We need to find all the subnormal numbers, that means b_0 must be zero while at least one of bs are nonzero, also, e must be the minimum.

For the largest possible number, we need to maximize significand, which means that all the b-s up to b_{p-1} must be $\beta$ -1s 

That gives us:
$N_{\max}^s = (0.(\beta-1)_1(\beta-1)_2...(\beta-1)_{p-1})_\beta \times {\beta}^{\check{e}} $

For the smallest positive number, we need to minimze the rest of the bs in a way that it is not all zeros, which gives us zeros for all the bs except for the very last one which will be the smallest positive number 1:

$N_{\min}^s = (0.0_10_2...1_{p-1})_\beta \times {\beta}^{\check{e}} $
